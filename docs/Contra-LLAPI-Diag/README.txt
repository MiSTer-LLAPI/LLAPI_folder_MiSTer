Contra Enhanced - LLAPI P1 timing diagnostic

Extract MiSTer-install.zip at the root of the MiSTer SD card. Use the same legally obtained Contra ROM as your normal installation. Launch Contra - LLAPI P1 Timing in the LLAPI Enhanced arcade folder and select LLAPI 2P. Connect the pad to P1. Close the OSD and play, then open P1 TIMING to view the last complete measurement.

Original FPGA core: Jotego and contributors. Enhanced integration: UltimateMiSTer.com (RickDangerous). LLAPI driver: Kitrinx and BlisSTer LLAPI Enhanced contributors. The separate report artifact contains the raw Quartus reports, instrumented sources and provenance. The exact workflow links its pinned base checkout and source overlays:
